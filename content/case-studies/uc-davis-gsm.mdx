<Intro>
Dean Unnava of the UC Davis Graduate School of Management, committed to supporting the lifelong professional success of alumni, aimed to extend community and educational access through a centralized platform.

The GSM could offer alumni:

- Professional certificates
- GSM Course content
</Intro>

<Section label="Research">

<div className="content-block">
### Research Methods

<div className="caption-block">
<p className="label">Focus Groups</p>

Alumni expectations for career support, content relevance, and community connection.
</div>

<div className="caption-block">
<p className="label">User Interviews</p>

Upskilling habits, learning formats, and GSM content credibility.
</div>

<div className="caption-block">
<p className="label">Quantitative Survey</p>

Platforms used, willingness to pay, pricing preferences, and content access.
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-block">
### Key Insights

1. Alumni want a **centralized platform** to access high-quality learning content and explore relevant GSM courses.
2. **Professional networking** is highly valued — users seek easier ways to connect with fellow alumni beyond personal circles.
3. There is a strong desire to **stay competitive** and up-to-date in their industries through **credible, time-efficient learning**.
4. Alumni engagement is low when communication is poor or last-minute — users want to feel seen, informed, and acknowledged.
</div>

</Section>

<Section label="The Opportunity">

Our research revealed that the core need goes beyond learning alone:

<div className="content-block">
### Learning alone isn't the core issue

<p className="hmw">→ How might we design a platform that prioritizes community, professional development, and meaningful alumni connection — not just access to content?</p>
</div>

<div className="content-block">
### Current alumni support is limited

<p className="hmw">→ How might we empower alumni to support one another and co-create a stronger, self-sustaining network?</p>
</div>

<div className="content-block">
### We're not the first lifelong learning platform

<p className="hmw">→ How might we differentiate by creating a holistic alumni experience that blends high-quality content with real connection and career growth?</p>
</div>

<ProblemBox>
How might we create a centralized platform where alumni and students can access high-quality learning content, engage in meaningful professional conversations, and continue to foster a strong, supportive GSM community for lifelong growth?
</ProblemBox>

</Section>

<Section label="Design">

The GSM Lifelong Learning Platform was structured around its unique audience. We aimed to go beyond a content hub by personalizing the experience for alumni, GSM content, and admin users.

<div className="content-block">
### My designs

Of all the features, I designed the following frames:

- Login Landing Page
- Onboarding Learning Preference Quiz
- Learning Content
- Saved Learning Content
- Alumni Directory
- Alumni Location Geomap
- Upload Content (Article, Video, & Podcast)
- Alumni-posted Content

My design partner spearheaded the onboarding profile quiz, discussion forum, messaging, and alumni profile.
</div>

<Section label="Reflection">

This project stretched my thinking, sharpened my skills, and made me even more excited to grow as a product designer.

<div className="content-block">
### What challenged me was …

<div className="caption-block">
<p className="label">Prototyping limits in Figma.</p>

Creating an advanced filter system pushed Figma to its edge. Without conditionals or multiple actions, I couldn't fully simulate the real interaction, which impacted usability testing.
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-block">
### I learned that …

<div className="caption-block">
<p className="label">PMs are magic.</p>

Watching our PMs in action was eye-opening. From organizing roadmaps in Notion to feature scoping and aligning the team, I left this project with a deep respect for cross-functional collaboration.
</div>
</div>

<div className="content-block">
### Accomplishments
