<Intro>
This self-directed project, worked on with my teammate Kayla Ho, was a chance to explore BeReal through the lens of both product opportunity and user needs. Kayla led research and I led design.

My goal was to strengthen my product ownership mindset and sharpen technical skills across research synthesis, prototyping, and storytelling — all while designing within the unique boundaries of a real social platform.
</Intro>

<Section label="Background Research">

<div className="content-block">
### Understanding BeReal's Mission

Before exploring feature ideas, I took time to understand what BeReal stands for, how it's grown, and what makes it stand out from other social platforms.
</div>

- BeReal isn't built for content creation or public performance. Its goal is to offer a more intentional, low-pressure way to connect with close friends.
- One thing that stood out to me was how intentional the restrictions are. They encourage users to post in the moment, without overthinking or curating.

<Quote label="ON LIMITED POSTING">
"By requiring simple and limited posting, the app tackles what most people see as the biggest problem with social media: fake and curated content."

[uxplanet.org — The case for BeReal](https://uxplanet.org/the-case-for-bereal-3ec81224a37)
</Quote>

</Section>

<Section label="Research">

To explore where BeReal could grow next, we studied how users interact with the app and what they value most. My teammate Kayla led the research plan and developed the interview and survey structure. I conducted research alongside her and helped synthesize findings that shaped our feature direction.

<div className="content-block">
### Research Goals

- Understand the user journey on BeReal
- Identify motivations behind using the app
- Explore behaviors and habits related to BeReal and other social media platforms
</div>

</Section>

<Section label="Quantitative Survey">

A survey with 210 respondents was used to gather behavioral insights and feature perceptions.

<div className="content-block">
### Key Findings

Most respondents were current BeReal users.

<CardRow columns="3" spaced="true">
  <StatCard value="210">respondents surveyed</StatCard>
  <StatCard value="86%">actively use BeReal</StatCard>
  <StatCard value="13%">rank it in their top three</StatCard>
</CardRow>

- Top platforms: Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat
- Most users report spending 3+ hours a day on social media
- BeReal is valued for its authenticity and nostalgic value
- Flagged for improvement: notification timing, limited interaction options, and no archive for old posts/comments
</div>

</Section>

<Section label="Insight Summary">

<ProblemBox>
BeReal effectively communicates its core value of authenticity, but struggles to maintain consistent user engagement in a competitive landscape. While the app resonates emotionally with users, it remains secondary to more established platforms.
</ProblemBox>

Any potential feature extensions must preserve the app's core strengths, simplicity, spontaneity, and honest connection, without increasing pressure or complexity.

</Section>

<Section label="Personas">

<PersonaRow

<Section label="Final Designs">

Three features made it through to hi-fi. Each had to expand what people could express without adding pressure to post.

</Section>

<FeatureCard
  label="GIF REACTIONS"
  title="GIF Reactions"
  mp4="/images/case-studies/bereal/gif-reaction.mp4"
  webm="/images/case-studies/bereal/gif-reaction.webm"
  poster="/images/case-studies/bereal/gif-reaction-poster.jpg"
>
React to your friends' BeReals with a GIF that captures your real-time energy — from clapping to cringing.

Mark, our passive user, wants low-effort ways to stay engaged without the pressure of posting. GIF Reactions gives him exactly that, a way to respond in the moment without needing to perform.
</FeatureCard>

<FeatureCard
  label="PUBLIC MEMORIES"
  title="Public Memories"
  mp4="/images/case-studies/bereal/public-memories.mp4"
  webm="/images/case-studies/bereal/public-memories.webm"
  poster="/images/case-studies/bereal/public-memories-poster.jpg"
>
Choose which past BeReals to show on your profile. A look back that still feels casual and true to you.

This directly answers a finding from our survey: BeReal is valued for its nostalgic value, being able to look back at old BeReals.
</FeatureCard>

<FeatureCard
  label="FRIENDS' MEMORIES"
  title="View Friends' Memories"
  mp4="/images/case-studies/bereal/friends-memories.mp4"
  webm="/images/case-studies/bereal/friends-memories.webm"
  poster="/images/case-studies/bereal/friends-memories-poster.jpg"
>
See a friend's public BeReals all in one place. Catch up on their life without needing a daily ping.

If your own memories are public, your friends' should be too. Friends' Memories is the other half of that idea, letting people revisit an authentic, unpolished diary of the people around them, not just their own.
</FeatureCard>
