Ticketmaster's Support Community is a platform for B2B clients (think live event professionals who lead the box-office at your fav venue), with end-to-end event-related resources to troubleshoot, learn, and train their staff.

My team, the Global Education team, lead product education standards for clients and internal employees.

The product education guide is hosted on [Ticketmaster's Support Community](https://business.ticketmaster.com/ai-powered-support-community/), providing clients with how-to articles, workflows, and best practices across all major Ticketmaster tools and features.

<Section label="Context">

<div className="content-block">

### Overview

Working on the Product Education guide challenged me to create a new framework around existing content. I collaborated with the team to deliver a transformed client experience and enforce an internal product education standard. The ask was to reimagine client's utilization of resources through a process-based model while considering internal processes of product education article creation or updates.

</div>

<div className="content-block">

### My Role

I led the design exploration, development, and delivered an interactive product guide, along with an internal style guide and content strategy documentation.

I worked closely with senior instructional designer, Chelsea Vivier, who served as the subject matter expert on the global product education standard and framework. We worked with other product team members that provided internal CMS workflow insight.

</div>

</Section>

<Section label="Pain Points">

<div className="content-block">

### Clients

<div className="caption-block">

<p className="label">under utilization of resources</p>

Many describe the current product education page as "death by links". 80% of clients I spoke to said they rarely use the Product Education pages and mainly use search.

Clients are busy. When clients seek out resources, they want to learn and to solve their problems quickly.

</div>

<Quote label="BUSINESS GOAL">
  "We need a way to breakdown products at a high level and allow clients to move through information in an easier way and encourage resource utilization." – Product Leader
</Quote>

</div>

<MockupImage src="/images/product-education-guide/pain-points-clients.png" alt="The current Product Education page, showing a dense grid of links clients described as 'death by links'" width={1326} height={1326} />

<div className="content-block">

### Product Teams

<div className="caption-block">

<p className="label">OPTIMIZE resource updates</p>

The current workflow to update resources is two-fold. First, internal teams must make updates to the original source files (original Google doc, videos, etc.) and roll it out to 14 different markets. After, they must go into the Salesforce CMS to update the content within the Salesforce "articles" and publish it.

</div>

<div className="caption-block">

<p className="label">CREATING NEW RESOURCES</p>

There are multiple teams that create product education resources, to optimize the creation workflow I worked with the team to align on a content strategy to apply across all products.

</div>

<Quote label="BUSINESS DIRECTION">
  "Right now we have many resource types on the platform (PDFs, articles, web links, etc.). But with the new framework, we hope to aggregate all content into articles." - SVP of Global Education
</Quote>

</div>

<MockupImage src="/images/product-education-guide/pain-points-product-teams.png" alt="Salesforce CMS content table showing product education articles across markets and languages" width={1326} height={990} />

</Section>

<Section label="Project Brief">

The Product Education Guide most be a loose, scalable guide that every product should have. Clients should see a breakdown of one product. Product teams should know the minimum requirements a product should have.

<ProblemBox>

How can we improve the product education resources to help clients *learn* about products more *effectively*, while ensuring *global* and *internal* *standardization*?

</ProblemBox>

</Section>

<Section label="Design">

I first aligned with instructional designers for requirements gathering, then I researched UI pattern for content heavy systems, gathered inspiration, and began experimenting.

<div className="content-block">

### Design Direction

After much research on different support sites, we decided to go with a shell layout, specifically with a left side navigation panel, a main content area, and a table of contents right as anchor links.

</div>

</Section>

<Wide>

<div className="content-block">

### Creating design components

Using the existing brand guidelines, I designed a system focused on **simplicity** and **consistency**. We prioritized article components utilized across all resources, ensuring **scalability** and **flexibility** across product and market needs.

</div>

<div className="content-block">

### Internal Improvements

After the design phase, I partnered closely with the instructional designer to write a style guide on how to ensure cohesiveness in future articles created. We also created a reusable Salesforce CMS article template for internal employees to quickly create new articles following the new standard.

</div>
